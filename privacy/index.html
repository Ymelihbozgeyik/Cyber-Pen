CyberPen
Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 19, 2026

Data collection

CyberPen does not have user accounts, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send your drawings or usage activity to our servers. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data with any third party.

Your drawings

Everything you draw with CyberPen — strokes and shapes — stays in the app's memory on your Mac for the current session only. Nothing is written to disk, synced, or uploaded anywhere; quitting CyberPen or clearing the canvas discards it.

Purchases

The CyberPen Pro unlock is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. CyberPen never sees or stores your payment details — Apple processes and stores that information under its own privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children's privacy

CyberPen does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, this applies equally to users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
